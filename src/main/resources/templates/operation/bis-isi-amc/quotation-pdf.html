BIS SOP of AMC and SIT

  1. In-process Quality Control – Monitoring product quality during manufacturing.
  2. Routine Product Testing – Regular testing of products as per Indian Standards (IS).
  3. Testing Frequency Compliance – Performing tests at defined intervals (lot-wise/daily/monthly).
  4. Equipment Calibration – Periodic calibration of testing equipment.
  5. Product Marking & Labelling Control – Using ISI mark only on approved products.
  6. Production & Consignee Records – Maintaining production and Consignee details.

EVTL- Scope of Work

  1. Documentation Support – Preparation of technical files and guidance on manuals and related documentation.
  2. Product Testing – Verification of in-house laboratory reports and coordination of product testing as per the prescribed testing frequency.
  3. SIT Guidance – Provide support and guidance during third-party inspections conducted by SGS and TUV.
  4. SIT Report & Corrective Actions – Review SIT reports and recommend corrective actions, wherever required.
  5. License Renewal Support – Preparation and submission of applications for BIS license renewal.
  6. Monthly/Quarterly Production & Consignee Data – Maintenance of production records, consignee details, and batch-wise production data.
  7. Training & updates – Training employees on BIS compliance and provide updates on latest BIS regulations.
